Critical Warnings for Aminoglycosides
Nephrotoxicity:
Dose-related renal damage. Monitor serum creatinine every 2-3 days.
Ototoxicity:
Irreversible hearing loss and vestibular damage. More common with prolonged therapy.
Route Specific:
-
Clinical Monitoring Guidelines
• Monitor peak (30 min post-infusion) and trough (pre-dose) levels
• Check serum creatinine every 2-3 days during therapy
• Adjust dose based on CrCl changes
• Avoid concurrent nephrotoxic drugs (vancomycin, NSAIDs, contrast media)
• Consider alternative antibiotics if CrCl < 30 mL/min
